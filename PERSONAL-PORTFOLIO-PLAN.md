# Your separate personal portfolio

PFZ craft is your art business website. Your personal portfolio can show you as a computer science student, developer, designer, and artist.

Suggested sections:

1. About me: short introduction and areas you work in.
2. Skills: programming, databases, design tools, art and craft.
3. Projects: cards showing the project name, your contribution, technologies, a screenshot, and links.
4. Certificates & Learning: course/training certificates, issuing organization, date, and verification links.
5. Achievements: competitions, participation, awards, and events, where relevant.
6. Contact: professional email, GitHub, and other chosen profiles.

Project links:

- Smart Dustbin: GitHub repository, a description, pictures, and a demo video. Hardware/PHP projects do not require a Vercel live link.
- PFZ craft: live Vercel website and its GitHub source repository.
- Other completed projects: include GitHub, a live demo if available, or screenshots/video if a live deployment is not appropriate.

Course certificate links go under Certificates & Learning. Use a button such as "Verify certificate" when the issuer provides an official verification URL. If there is no verification URL, offer "View certificate" linked to an image or PDF. Do not call an ordinary image link a verification link.

Art-related certificates can also appear on PFZ craft. General programming/course certificates usually belong in your personal portfolio. A relevant certificate can appear in both places.

We have prepared PFZ craft in this package. The separate personal portfolio has not yet been built.
